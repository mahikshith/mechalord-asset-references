# Iron Front v3: lanes, pressure and earned rewards

## Evidence and limits

Reviewed the user-supplied Shorts again through the browser on 2026-10-04. These are third-party uploads, including advertising reviews, not verified recordings of every level shipped in Top Lords. They establish visual references; they do not establish retention, purchase conversion or representative player experience.

- [Top Lords by KonstantinAkentjev](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/Ae5l4XBF9nw): compact blue player formation in the lower third, dense approaching red troops, large leader among small troops, long reinforcement gate chain at the side, numbered barriers. The pressure remains visible beyond the immediate interaction.
- [Top War advertising review, level 601](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/PrVaQhZvJbk): two adjacent multiplication gates, a stronger reward behind a numbered shooting barrier, oversized red roller with light spikes, densely occupied winding routes. The large numerical reward is advertising evidence, not a balance target for this prototype.
- [Top War advertising review](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/TSW6ULkR-J0): divided bridge choices, contrasting favorable/unfavorable gates, larger champion figures within a dense blue army, incoming roller and red army. Previously reviewed the same clip in this session.
- [Top War Part 118](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/9tyfoGxjpx0): previous session observations recorded in top-lords-rebuild-study.md; side gate chains and high crowd density.

## Application to the current build

1. Keep the existing original Mechalord commander and setting. Enlarge troop representatives and the commander while preserving a visible forward threat area.
2. Use explicit paired left/right gate apertures, clear operation labels, and one activation per logical gate. A painted road divider supports lane reading without implying a wall that does not collide.
3. Make rollers visibly wider, red with contrasting metal spikes, and render their actual moving collision width.
4. Separate three authored encounter sequences: reinforcement decisions on Relic Causeway, moving hazards in Roller Foundry, heavier shooting barriers and ranged threats in Citadel Breach. Give them distinct environment palettes and roadside props.
5. Earn weapon experience by breaking crates; four tiers have explicit requirements. Relic charge starts partially filled and is replenished through combat, rather than offering an immediate free activation.
6. Use a larger armored Reaver to communicate elite threats and a new articulated Forge Tyrant silhouette for the boss. These are original modeled interpretations of generated references, not image-to-3D conversions.
7. Launch actual incoming shells, rockets and energy orbs. Warning areas precede attacks; projectiles have travel time and can miss. Shield/EMP/Overdrive change the response rather than replacing steering.
8. Keep completed levels replayable and preserve best scores locally. No payment or purchase requirement is introduced in this build.

## Validation boundaries

The current deliverable remains a browser playtest using a C++ simulation compiled to WebAssembly. It is not an Android package or a completed Unreal integration. More visible representatives increase rendering cost; desktop observation is not evidence of 30 fps on Nothing Phone (3), and older-phone support remains unverified. The art and encounter design require player feedback before claims about quality or engagement.
